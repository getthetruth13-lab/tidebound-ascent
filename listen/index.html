Tidebound sound picks (LISTEN-1101)

32 sounds × 3 free options (A/B/C), all CC0. "now" plays the file the game uses today. Every option is trimmed, mono 48 kHz, filtered 65 Hz–9 kHz and leveled to the game's mix (true peak −1.5 dB). Use headphones or phone speaker at your normal game volume. The picks were chosen from names, descriptions and measurements, not by ear, so trust your ears. Freesound clips are the public 128k preview mp3s (the lossless originals need a login, and I'd download them before shipping). Nothing here ships until you pick. The game doesn't link to this page.

Download the single-file version (zip, 1.2 MB). It works offline once unzipped. Licenses: SOURCES.csv and license-notes/.

Act 2–3 enemy signature sounds have their own set from 10-31 (N1–N16), which isn't repeated here.
